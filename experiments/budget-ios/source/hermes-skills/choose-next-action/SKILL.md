# Choose next action

Read `process/action-selection.md` and project memory. Consider at least two feasible actions when the decision is nontrivial. Prefer restoring evidence, resolving blockers, repairing regressions, closing a valuable result, or strengthening a high-risk proof.

Record considered actions, the chosen action, expected evidence and rationale in `telemetry/run-log.md`. Do not choose an action outside `process/autonomy-policy.md`; escalate one concrete decision instead.
