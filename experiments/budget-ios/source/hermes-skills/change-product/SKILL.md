# Change product

Work on one active result at a time. Read its criteria, dependencies, unresolved risks and applicable product rules before changing code. Keep the change bounded and add or update checks that establish expected behavior.

Move the result to `evidence-pending` when implementation is complete. Record assumptions and incomplete evidence; do not mark the result proved or accepted yourself.
